Dataset files (KDDTrain+.txt, KDDTest+.txt) are not included here due to size.
Download the standard NSL-KDD files and place them in this data/ folder:
https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/nsl.html
or any NSL-KDD GitHub mirror providing KDDTrain+.txt / KDDTest+.txt in the
standard 41-feature comma-separated format (with or without the trailing
difficulty column -- src/data_loading.py auto-detects both).
